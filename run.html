WHAT YOU ARE RUNNING

A real Linux live session

This boots the Debian-based Restore Lab live system with XFCE, Firefox ESR and the Calamares graphical installer. Its Windows-inspired setup screens are an optional visual simulator, not Microsoft Windows.

BROWSER REQUIREMENTS

Keep the browser open

The virtual PC uses your computer's CPU and memory while this tab is running. On a 4 GB computer, close extra tabs and apps first. Boot speed depends on your device and internet connection.

SAFETY & STORAGE

Separate from your real disks

The VM boots from the ISO as a virtual CD and uses only its temporary virtual disk. This page does not access, format or install anything to your real internal drives or USB devices.
